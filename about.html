About

I'm Publius Nakamoto, born and raised in the Pacific Northwest. I take courses from Peterson Academy — history, philosophy, science, economics — and turn each one into something more useful than a stack of lecture notes.

How this works

For every course, the lecture transcripts, readings, and my own notes get turned into:

  • An overview video for a fast first pass
  • A slide deck for quick review before I forget the details
  • A deep-dive podcast for long drives through the evergreens
  • A study guide with the questions I actually get wrong

The Peterson Academy page collects each course as it's finished. Some are complete, some are still missing a piece or two — that's the honest state of the work.
