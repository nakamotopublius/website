Mobile information is not shared for marketing (required disclosure)

We do not share, sell, rent, transfer, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Consent for this messaging program is collected only for Gary Anderson / Boss on +1 (425) 642-5911 and is not transferable to other brands, affiliates, or lead generators.

Who we are and what this program is

Gary Anderson (sole proprietor) operates a transactional, two-way SMS program on +1 (425) 642-5911. People Gary has personally given the number may text an AI assistant named Boss for operational help (for example scheduling questions, status checks, or instructions) and receive operational replies. Messages are processed by that AI assistant to generate those replies.

This program is not used for marketing, promotions, advertising, contests, surveys for hire, or affiliate / third-party lead generation.

Related terms: https://publiusnakamoto.com/terms

Information we collect

When you text or otherwise participate in this SMS program, we may collect and process:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • The content of messages you send and replies you receive
  • Basic delivery and carrier metadata (for example timestamps, delivery status, carrier error codes)
  • Opt-in and opt-out status and related timestamps
  • HELP / STOP and similar keyword requests

We do not intentionally collect payment card numbers, government IDs, or other sensitive identity documents through this SMS channel. Please do not text secrets you do not want logged.

How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • Receive inbound texts and send operational replies for this program
  • Honor STOP, HELP, and related carrier keywords
  • Prevent abuse, debug delivery failures, and maintain security
  • Meet legal, carrier, and messaging-platform compliance obligations for A2P 10DLC / transactional SMS

Message frequency varies based on your inbound texts and the assistant’s operational replies. Message and data rates may apply.

How we obtain consent (opt-in)

Consent is given when you:

  • Voluntarily text +1 (425) 642-5911 after receiving the number directly from Gary Anderson (verbal or written share), or
  • Text START, YES, or UNSTOP after previously opting out

Consent is specific to this program and brand. It is not buried in unrelated contracts and is not sold or shared for others’ marketing.

Opt-out and help
  • Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT to cancel. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages from this number unless you opt back in.
  • Reply HELP or INFO, or email gary@garyanderson.net, for help.
Service providers (processing only — not for their marketing)

To run this program we use infrastructure providers that process data solely to deliver the service, under contracts or terms that do not allow them to use your mobile number or SMS consent for their own marketing. Examples include:

  • The SMS / telephony platform that carries messages (currently Twilio)
  • AI inference providers used to generate assistant replies (currently xAI / related tooling)
  • Hosting and logging systems used to operate and debug the assistant

Those providers act as processors for this program. They are not given permission to sell or use your messaging consent for marketing. The non-sharing statement in the section above still applies.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share SMS opt-in lists with affiliates or lead generators.

Data retention

Message logs and related metadata are retained only as long as needed to operate, secure, and debug the assistant and to meet legal or carrier requirements, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed. Opt-out records may be kept longer so we do not message you again by mistake.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a small sole-proprietor SMS program (access controls, secrets handling, and limited logging). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children’s privacy

This program is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect SMS information from children under 13. If you believe a child has texted this number, contact gary@garyanderson.net and we will delete the relevant data where feasible.

Your choices and requests

You may opt out via STOP keywords as above. For access, deletion, or other privacy requests related to this SMS program, email gary@garyanderson.net. We will respond within a reasonable time as required by applicable law.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at https://publiusnakamoto.com/privacy with a new effective date. Material changes that affect how we handle mobile consent will not reduce the non-sharing commitment above without a clear update on this page.
